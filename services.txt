1. Barangay Clearance
Purpose: A document used to certify that a resident is known in the barangay and has no recorded issue that would prevent its issuance.
- Requirements: Valid ID, proof of residency, and completed application form.
- Processing: Submit requirements to the barangay office and wait for verification.
- Fee: Depends on barangay regulations.
- Where to apply: Barangay Hall.


2. Certificate of Residency
Purpose: A document that confirms a person is currently residing in a particular barangay.
- Requirements: Valid ID, proof of address, and completed application form.
- Processing: Submit the requirements for verification of residency.
- Fee: Depends on barangay regulations.
- Where to apply: Barangay Hall.
3. Certificate of Indigency

Purpose: A document certifying that a resident belongs to a low-income or financially disadvantaged household. It may be used when applying for certain assistance programs.
- Requirements: Valid ID, proof of residency, and other supporting documents if required.
- Processing: Submit an application for assessment and verification by barangay officials.
- Fee: Depends on barangay regulations.
- Where to apply: Barangay Hall.
